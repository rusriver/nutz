This is similar to controlflow/factory, but with these differences:

    1) The conceptual model is rather simple - it's just a sequence of stations.

    2) It's buildable forward - i.e. not constant - new stations, and subsequences
        of stations can be added while riding the existing part of the road. The analogy
        is building a raiload further, where the direction of that build depends
        on current and previous conditions.
